🔑 How to Get Your Device UID

Your Device UID is a unique hardware token used to authorize transactions when fingerprint sensors are unavailable.

Where to Find Your UID:

  • Android Settings: Navigate to Settings > About Phone > Status / Device ID.
  • Terminal / Termux: Execute getprop ro.serialno or inspect device hardware UUID.
  • Saved Hardware Key: Enter your registered application hardware token.

Paste or type your UID into the text field above, then tap "2b. Submit Device UID Token" to proceed.
