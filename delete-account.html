How to Request Full Account Deletion

If you wish to permanently delete your Connectgold game account, player profile, and all associated cloud data, you can submit a request using the method below:

  • Email Request: Send an email to our support team at pinchasdegreat@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  • Please include your registered sign-in email address, player ID, or display name so we can locate your account.

Upon verifying your ownership, we will permanently delete your account profile, high scores, leaderboards, level progression, and associated cloud telemetry within 30 days.

What Happens When Your Account is Deleted?

  • Your player profile and handle are permanently removed from Google Play Games and our servers.
  • All cloud-synchronized game progress, level high scores, and win streaks are deleted.
  • Any unspent in-game coins, hints, or consumable rewards are forfeited.
  • Your game license and purchase history remain governed by the respective app store (Google Play / Amazon Appstore).
