How to Request Data Deletion

We respect your privacy and provide straightforward methods to delete your game account and all associated cloud data:

  • Method 1 (In-App): You can clear all local game data, high scores, and preferences directly from your device settings by going to Settings > Apps > Connectgold > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  • Method 2 (Email Request): You can submit a formal account and data deletion request directly to our support team.

When requesting data deletion via email, please provide your player ID or registered sign-in email address. Upon verification, we will permanently delete your cloud profile, high scores, level progression, and analytics telemetry within 30 days.

What Data is Deleted?

When your account deletion request is processed, the following information is permanently removed:

  • Player profile and display name
  • Cloud-synchronized level progression, high scores, and win streaks
  • In-game coin balances and unspent consumable rewards
  • Multiplayer presence and telemetry history
