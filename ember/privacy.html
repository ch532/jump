🔥 Ember AS Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Non-Custodial Architecture

Ember AS operates strictly as a non-custodial Bitcoin wallet infrastructure. Private keys are encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM prior to storage or memory processing.

2. Data Collection & Privacy

We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit unencrypted private keys, recovery seed phrases, or personally identifiable information (PII) to external parties.

3. Network Interaction

To deliver real-time on-chain balances and broadcast signed PSBT hex payloads, public Bitcoin wallet addresses interact exclusively with public blockchain API nodes.

4. Contact & Developer Details

For support or verification, visit chyke.online.
