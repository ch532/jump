Privacy Policy & Legal Notice

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how telemetry, spatial inputs, and analytical parameters are processed when using the Autonomous Macro-Environmental AI Engine web application.

1. Location Telemetry & Input Parameters

To compute real-time localized microclimate, traffic, and urban evaluations, the application processes geographical spatial parameters:

  • Device Coordinates: When you grant browser location permissions, precise latitude and longitude coordinates are temporarily used to evaluate your immediate environment.
  • Location Searches: Text queries for city or regional names are dynamically resolved into geographic coordinates using secure external geocoding services.
  • Automated Network Location: If no explicit target location is selected, approximate network-level IP geolocation is used as a fallback to retrieve regional atmospheric conditions.
  • Telemetry Inputs: User-configured diagnostic settings (such as traffic velocity, congestion levels, population density, public sentiment markers, and urban classification parameters) are transmitted strictly for diagnostic evaluation.

2. Data Processing & AI Diagnostic Infrastructure

Your inputs are processed through secure edge infrastructure and remote analytical engines to generate diagnostic evaluations:

  • Real-Time Environmental Data Feeds: Target coordinates are used to fetch live public atmospheric quality metrics, emissions levels, and surface meteorological feeds from global weather networks.
  • Edge Computing & AI Inference: Multi-domain telemetry inputs are compiled and evaluated serverlessly via secure edge AI gateways and remote large language model infrastructure.
  • Client-Side Audio Output: Voice diagnostic readouts are synthesized locally within your web browser using native device text-to-speech capabilities. Audio data is never transmitted to external servers.

3. Data Protection & Retention

All environmental evaluations and AI inference requests are calculated dynamically in real time. We do not track, profile, or store your real-world personal identity, nor do we retain permanent historical databases of your GPS coordinates or custom evaluation inputs.

4. Multi-Purpose Usage Notice

This engine is constructed for multi-purpose applications, including scientific research modeling, educational spatial analytics, urban environmental study, and exploratory evaluation globally. All generated insights are for analytical and informational purposes.
