Privacy Policy for Connectgold

Effective Date: November 8, 2026 | Application: Connectgold (com.atzec.app)

Welcome to Connectgold ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, transparent gaming environment. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application Connectgold (com.atzec.app).

1. Information We Collect

When you play Connectgold, we may process certain information to provide game services, cloud saves, multiplayer matchmaking, and stability improvements:

  • Google Account Information: If you sign in using Google Sign-In, we process your public profile details (Display Name, Email Address, and User ID) to enable cloud saves, match progress synchronization, and multiplayer profiles.
  • Gameplay & Saved Data: Level progress, high scores, remaining lives, coins, win-streak crowns, and settings preferences.
  • Multiplayer Chat Messages: Text messages sent during online multiplayer matches or in the Global Lobby. Chat messages are processed on-device via Google ML Kit for translation and content moderation.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers, and crash diagnostic logs via Firebase Crashlytics to fix performance bugs.
  • App Performance & Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics collected via Firebase Analytics and Mixpanel to optimize puzzle difficulty and game features.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Connectgold integrates trusted third-party SDKs to support game functionality, analytics, and advertising. These services operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: Provides Google Sign-In authentication, cloud database storage, push notifications, and crash diagnostics. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Standalone Unity Ads: Displays video ads and rewarded ad placements (Game ID: 5962661). Unity Ads may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data to serve relevant ads. (Unity Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Integrity API: Verifies app authenticity and prevents unauthorized tampering or piracy.
  • Mixpanel Analytics: Measures feature engagement and level completion events. (Mixpanel Privacy Policy)

3. User Generated Content & Moderation

Connectgold provides real-time online multiplayer chat. To maintain a safe and respectful community in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • In-game chat is moderated using automated on-device filtering to block hate speech, vulgarity, and personal information doxxing.
  • Players can report or block inappropriate users at any time using the in-game moderation tools.

4. Children's Privacy

Connectgold is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Your game progress is linked to your Google Account or stored locally on your device. You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can clear your local data or sign out of your Google Account via the in-game Settings menu.
  • Data Deletion Request: To request full deletion of your cloud save profile and multiplayer records, please email us at support@connectgold.app with your account details.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to secure all data transmissions between your device, Firebase Realtime Database, and Google Cloud services.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in game features or legal regulations. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
