Connectgold Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Connectgold ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit our website at chyke.online or use Connectgold and its supported services.

We aim to collect only information needed to operate, secure, maintain, and improve our services. Our actual data practices depend on the features you use and the services enabled in the website or application.

1. Information We May Collect

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, we may process information such as your name, email address, account identifier, and information you submit through forms or when contacting support.

Sign-in information

If you sign in using a supported provider, such as Google or GitHub, that provider may share basic profile information and account identifiers with us according to your authorization and the provider's privacy settings. We do not receive your provider password through a standard third-party sign-in flow.

Device and technical information

Depending on the services enabled, we or our service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, app version, device identifiers, diagnostic records, timestamps, and security events.

Usage and analytics information

If analytics or diagnostic tools are enabled, information about interactions with our services may be collected to understand performance, identify errors, measure usage, and improve reliability.

Media and downloads

When you choose to play media, enter a streaming address, or download content, the application processes the information needed to perform that action. Streaming providers and remote servers may receive your IP address, connection details, and requests necessary to deliver the content.

Local media files selected from your device may be accessed to provide the playback or download feature you request. Whether file information leaves your device depends on the feature and service used.

Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, the relevant app store or payment provider processes the transaction. We may receive subscription status, product identifiers, transaction references, or other information needed to verify access to premium features. Payment credentials are handled by the applicable payment provider.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain accounts and sign-in sessions.
  • Provide media playback, streaming, and download features.
  • Verify subscriptions and manage access to premium features.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website and application.
  • Diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and protect account security.
  • Respond to support requests and important service notices.
  • Measure usage and performance where analytics are enabled.
  • Display or measure advertising where advertising services are enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences, maintain sessions, measure performance, or support advertising where enabled.

Third-party services embedded in the website may use their own cookies or technologies under their respective policies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality. Where required, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

4. Advertising and Analytics

If advertising or analytics SDKs are integrated into our website or app, those providers may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, or advertising events, depending on their configuration and your choices.

These services may use information to deliver or measure ads, detect invalid activity, produce analytics, and improve their services. Where required, we provide disclosures and obtain consent before applicable data processing.

The providers actually used may vary by platform and release. Please consult the privacy notices of the third-party services enabled in the version you use.

5. How We Share Information

We do not disclose personal information indiscriminately. Information may be shared, when necessary, with:

  • Service providers: Hosting, authentication, analytics, diagnostics, security, and technical infrastructure providers supporting our services.
  • Advertising partners: Providers used to deliver, measure, or protect advertising where enabled.
  • Payment and app-store providers: Services that process transactions or verify subscriptions.
  • Content providers: Remote servers requested by you when you use streaming or online media features.
  • Legal and safety recipients: Where disclosure is legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, users, and service security.

We do not sell personal information for money. Any other disclosure or sharing is subject to applicable law and the choices available to you. Where a provider acts independently as a controller, its own privacy policy may also apply.

6. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored on your device or on systems operated by us or our service providers, depending on the feature. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks involved.

Safeguards may include access controls, secure connections, restricted access, and security monitoring where supported. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and meeting legal requirements.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the service involved. Information may be deleted, anonymized, or securely disposed of when it is no longer required, subject to lawful retention obligations.

If your account supports account deletion, use the available account controls or contact us using the details below to request deletion. We will explain any applicable limitations and process eligible requests in accordance with applicable law. Deleting an account may not automatically cancel a subscription managed by an app store; manage that subscription separately with the relevant provider.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

You may also be able to manage permissions through your device, sign-in permissions through your identity provider, and advertising or cookie preferences through the relevant settings. Contact us to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers and comply with applicable data protection requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where a service is directed to children or requires parental authorization, we will follow the legal requirements applicable to that service. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

11. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our services may contain links to third-party websites, streaming sources, sign-in providers, or other services. We do not control their privacy practices. Review their privacy policies before providing information or using their services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for material changes.
