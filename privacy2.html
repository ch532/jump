Privacy Policy for Connectgold

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Connectgold ("we," "our," or "the app") handles your data. Connectgold is built as an independent, local-first artificial intelligence assistant that prioritizes user privacy, transparency, and data sovereignty.

1. Data Collection & Information We Do Not Collect

We believe your personal data belongs to you. Connectgold operates without mandatory accounts:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Advertising Tracking: The app does not collect or track your Android Advertising ID (AAID) and serves no third-party behavioral ads.
  • No Location Data: We do not access, track, or store your GPS or network location.

2. API Keys & AI Processing (Bring Your Own Key)

Connectgold uses a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) architecture:

  • Your Google Gemini API key is stored securely on your local device using Android private app storage. It is never transmitted to our servers.
  • When you send prompts or attachments, they are routed directly from your device to the Google Gemini API endpoint over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Usage is governed by Google AI Studio Terms of Service.

3. Local Storage & Chat Sovereignty

  • On-Device Storage: All chat histories, task observations, and prompt sessions are kept strictly inside your local device database.
  • Manual Backup & Restore: You retain complete control over your conversation logs. You can export your chats as an unencrypted JSON file to your chosen directory (local storage, SD card, or personal cloud drive) and restore it at your own discretion.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Subscriptions (such as Pro Premium) are processed directly by the Google Play Billing system. We do not process, receive, or store your credit card, bank, or billing information. All transactions are handled securely by Google Play.

5. Third-Party Services

The app integrates standard Google Play libraries to maintain reliability and security:

  • Google Play In-App Updates & Reviews: Facilitates seamless client updates and rating prompts managed directly by Google Play.
  • Google Play Integrity API: Protects the application binary against tampering and fraudulent deployment.

6. Children’s Privacy

Connectgold does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The application does not collect personally identifiable information from any user.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published directly to this URL with an updated effective date.
