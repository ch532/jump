Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Welcome to Raregem Technology ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed while you access our global real-time environmental intelligence platform and edge AI services.

1. Information We Collect

Our platform is designed with data minimization in mind. When you interact with Raregem Technology, we may process:

  • Geospatial Coordinates: Latitude and longitude coordinates explicitly selected via map interaction or location search inputs to query environmental metrics.
  • Technical Telemetry: Standard browser header information, IP addresses, edge routing regions, and cache states processed securely via our Cloudflare worker infrastructure.

2. How We Use Information

The limited data collected is used solely to:

  • Fetch real-time atmospheric, meteorological, air quality, and NASA agro-meteorological data for your selected target zone.
  • Execute AI swarm reasoning and generate multi-domain intelligence reports.
  • Optimize edge gateway routing, speed, and overall platform reliability.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Geospatial queries are processed via secure open-source meteorological APIs and edge intelligence nodes necessary to deliver real-time calculations. All data transmissions utilize secure TLS protocols.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Raregem Technology does not utilize intrusive tracking cookies. We may use ephemeral local session storage solely to maintain your UI parameter preferences and recent coordinate states during an active browser session.

5. Security Measures

We implement robust industry-standard security protocols, including secure edge gateways, HTTPS encryption, and token validation to safeguard all requests processed through our network.
