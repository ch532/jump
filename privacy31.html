1. Introduction

Welcome to Connectgold Media. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting data processed through our 24x Remote AI Telephoto Camera system, edge network infrastructure, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations.

2. Data Collection & Processing

Connectgold Media operates on a local-first, edge-processing architecture designed to maximize user data sovereignty:

  • Camera Streams & Video Frames: Snapshot requests pass temporarily through ephemeral Cloudflare Workers to perform AI vision analysis. Video frames are held in memory strictly for the duration of the inference request and are not permanently stored on central databases.
  • Stream Configurations: Endpoint parameters, RTSP bridge snapshot URLs, and prompt queries reside locally on your client session or custom Worker environment variables.
  • Telemetry & Usage Data: Basic usage logs (IP address, request timestamps, and response status) may be processed transiently for diagnostic and rate-limiting purposes.

3. AI Model Inference

Image data submitted to the Connectgold Media AI vision engine is passed to Workers AI (e.g., Llama 3.2 Vision models) solely to process visual analysis prompts. Captured frames are not used to train global public AI models.

4. Third-Party Streams & Gateways

Users connecting external RTSP, ONVIF, or HTTP snapshot streams (e.g., go2rtc or local camera bridges) are responsible for ensuring that network endpoints and authentication credentials are appropriately secured.

5. Security

We employ standard transport-layer security (HTTPS/TLS) and edge security mechanisms to protect data in transit between your client interface, serverless workers, and camera endpoints.

6. Contact Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or platform integrations, contact Connectgold Media administration at chyke.online.
