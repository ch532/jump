Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Progold TV application ("the App"), developed for connected TV and mobile devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring full transparency regarding the operation of our services.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Progold TV is designed to function without requiring personal user accounts or identity verification. We do not collect, store, or sell any of the following:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number).
  • Precise or coarse GPS location data.
  • Contacts, media library files, or local device storage contents.
  • Biometric data or financial credentials.

2. In-App Purchases & Payment Data

All in-app purchases and subscription transactions (such as the Progold VIP Pass) are processed directly by the Amazon Appstore payment infrastructure. Progold TV does not process, handle, or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details. Transactions are governed by Amazon's Privacy Notice and Terms of Service.

3. Network Requests & Content Streaming

To deliver linear broadcast channels and fetch Electronic Program Guide (EPG) data, the App communicates with remote content servers via standard HTTP/HTTPS requests. These streaming requests inherently transmit standard technical header information (such as your IP address and standard video player user-agent) necessary to establish real-time media feeds.

4. Advertising & Telemetry

Non-subscribed tiers of the application may request standard digital video advertising (such as VAST tags). Ad delivery partners may receive standard non-personal device information (such as screen resolution and device type) solely for the technical delivery of video ads. Subscribers to the VIP Pass receive an ad-free experience.

5. Permissions Utilized

The application only requests permissions strictly essential for core media streaming functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to stream video feeds and fetch EPG channel lists.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify active network connectivity before streaming.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. All content delivery complies with general digital safety standards.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal updates. Any revisions will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy online.
