1. Introduction

Welcome to Connectgold ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile game.

2. Information We Collect

We integrate Huawei Mobile Services (HMS) to provide game features. Depending on your interactions, we may collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Open Device ID (ODID) and device configuration info for analytics and service delivery.
  • Analytics Data: Gameplay events, level progression, and app performance via Huawei HiAnalytics.
  • In-App Purchases: Transaction details processed securely through Huawei In-App Purchases (HMS IAP).
  • Advertising Data: Ad interaction metrics via Huawei Ads Kit to deliver rewarded and interstitial ads.
  • Contextual Data (Awareness Kit): Optional contextual insights (such as time, location, or weather) if granted permission to enhance gameplay dynamics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve game functionality across 1,000 levels and PvP arenas.
  • Process in-app purchases and award daily login rewards.
  • Display relevant advertisements and reward players for viewing promotional videos.
  • Analyze game performance and troubleshoot crashes using AppGallery Connect.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Your data is processed securely through Huawei Mobile Services (HMS) and AppGallery Connect infrastructure. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You can manage app permissions (such as location or notifications) anytime through your device settings. You may also request data deletion by contacting us.
