1. Introduction

Welcome to Connectgold ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile game (available on Google Play, Amazon Appstore, and other platforms) or visit our website at https://chyke.online.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following data:

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you sign in using Google Play Games Services, Login with Amazon, or play as a Guest, we collect basic profile identifiers (such as display name and player ID) to manage your game progress and leaderboards.
  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Level completions, high scores, win streaks, coin balances, and achievements to synchronize your game progress.
  • Device & Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network connection status.
  • Analytics & Performance Data: Crash reports, ANR logs, performance traces, and gameplay event analytics collected via Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance, and Mixpanel.
  • Advertising Data: Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data processed via Unity Ads / LevelPlay mediation partners to serve rewarded and interstitial advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services

We integrate trusted third-party SDKs that may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Play Games: Authentication, achievements, and leaderboards.
  • Amazon Appstore Services: In-App Purchasing (IAP), Login with Amazon (LWA), and Amazon Device Messaging (ADM).
  • Firebase & Google Cloud: Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, and Cloud Sync.
  • Unity Ads & LevelPlay Mediation: Video and interstitial advertisement delivery.
  • Ably Realtime: Real-time multiplayer rooms, presence, and match synchronization.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and provide the Connectgold game experience.
  • Synchronize your game progress, high scores, and leaderboards across sessions.
  • Process in-app purchases and reward offers securely.
  • Monitor app performance, diagnose crashes, and fix technical bugs.
  • Deliver relevant advertisements and measure ad performance.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Connectgold complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable family policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent or platform age signal verification.

6. Data Security

We implement robust security measures, including HTTPS encryption, secure cloud storage, and Google Play Integrity verification, to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated game data at any time. To request account deletion:

  • You can clear your local game data directly from your device settings (App Info > Clear Storage / Data).
  • You can contact us at pinchasdegreat@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", providing your player ID or sign-in email.
  • Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your cloud profile, high scores, level progress, and associated telemetry data within 30 days.

8. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You can reset your local game progress or request account data removal by contacting us.
