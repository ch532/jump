1. Overview

Connectgold is an app that helps users discover and manage Google Slides add-ons in one place. We use Google APIs to show your presentations and relevant add-ons.

2. Data Collection and Usage - What Data We Collect

A) Google User data:[STRIPPED]b>

  • Google Slides Data (slides.readonly): We collect titles and metadata of your Google Slides presentations to display them inside Connectgold and recommend relevant add-ons. We do NOT edit your slides without your explicit click.
  • Google Drive Data (drive.readonly): We collect file names and IDs of your Slides presentations stored in Drive to list them in the app.
  • Google Workspace Marketplace data:[STRIPPED]b> We collect public information about Google Slides add-ons (name, description, icon) to display them in Connectgold.

B) Personal Information:

  • Email address (if you contact support at chyke88@chyke88.cc.cd)
  • We do NOT collect passwords, payment cards, or sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To display your Google Slides inside Connectgold
  • To show and recommend relevant Google Slides add-ons like Slides Toolbox, Unsplash, Pear Deck
  • To improve Connectgold features and user experience
  • To provide customer support

We do NOT use your Google data for advertising, selling, or profiling.

4. How We Share Your Data

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your Google user data with third parties. We only share data if required by law or with your explicit consent.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Your Google Slides metadata is displayed temporarily and not stored permanently on our servers unless you save a project in Connectgold. If you delete your Connectgold account, we delete your associated data within 30 days.

6. Data Security

We use HTTPS, secure servers, and industry-standard security to protect your data. Access to Google data is via OAuth 2.0 and limited to the scopes you approve.

7. Google Limited Use Disclosure

Connectgold's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You can revoke Connectgold's access to your Google data at any time via https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can request deletion of your data by emailing chyke88@chyke88.cc.cd.

9. Children's Privacy

Connectgold is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
