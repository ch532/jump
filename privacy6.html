Privacy Policy for the Connectgold application and services

Effective date: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Prohub respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Connectgold application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the authentication method you use, Connectgold may receive information necessary to create and maintain your account.

This information may include:

  • Email address
  • Name or display name
  • Profile information provided by an authentication provider
  • Authentication identifiers
  • Information necessary to maintain your signed-in session

3. Social Authentication

Connectgold may allow users to authenticate using supported third-party authentication providers, including Google and X/Twitter.

When you choose a third-party authentication provider, that provider may share information with Connectgold according to the permissions and settings associated with your account and the authentication process.

Connectgold uses this information to authenticate your account and provide the requested application functionality.

4. Firebase Authentication

Connectgold uses Firebase Authentication to help manage user authentication and account credentials. Authentication information is processed through Firebase according to Google's applicable policies and terms.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected through Connectgold may be used to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide application features
  • Maintain application security
  • Prevent unauthorized access
  • Improve reliability and functionality
  • Respond to support requests

6. Information Sharing

Connectgold does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary for authentication, hosting, analytics, security, or operation of the application.

7. Third-Party Services

Connectgold may use third-party services such as Firebase and social authentication providers. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information associated with Connectgold. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

Account information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable requirements, or resolve disputes.

10. Account Deletion

If Connectgold provides an account deletion feature, users may use that feature to request deletion of their account and associated data. Where an in-app deletion feature is not available, users may contact us using the contact information below.

11. Children's Privacy

Connectgold is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that personal information has been collected improperly, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.
