Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Connectgold Creative ("we", "our", or "the service") handles information when you use our AI-powered product label generator and related website features.

By using Connectgold Creative, you acknowledge this policy. Please do not submit information you do not have permission to share.

1. Information You Provide

When creating product labels, you may enter information such as:

  • Product names, categories, descriptions and brand names.
  • Ingredients, materials and product features.
  • Quantity, price, batch number and expiry date.
  • Manufacturer, business contact or address details.
  • Design preferences and other information you choose to provide.

Only enter personal or business information that is necessary for your label. You are responsible for ensuring you have permission to use any information you submit.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted to the service may be used to:

  • Generate and format product label wording using AI.
  • Display your label preview in your browser.
  • Respond to requests and diagnose technical errors.
  • Protect the service against abuse and unauthorized use.
  • Maintain, secure and improve the service where applicable.

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Connectgold Creative uses a Cloudflare Worker to process label-generation requests. When you request AI-generated wording, the product information included in that request is sent to the configured Cloudflare Workers AI service for processing.

AI-generated text may be inaccurate or incomplete. Review all generated content before using it on a product. Do not rely on AI output alone for ingredient declarations, legal notices, safety information, certifications or regulatory compliance.

4. Printing and Browser Processing

Label previews are rendered in your browser. When you choose to print, the browser opens its print interface. Your selected printer, operating system or browser may process the print job according to its own privacy practices.

We do not claim that information is automatically erased from every browser, printer, device or service after printing.

5. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

The label studio code may not require cookies for its basic label-generation and printing functions. However, the wider Connect Gold website or its hosting, security, analytics, advertising or embedded services may use cookies or similar technologies.

Where such technologies are used, their purposes and applicable choices should be disclosed through the relevant website notices and consent controls. Third-party services may process technical information under their own privacy policies.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell the product information you enter into the label generator as a standalone data product. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the service, including Cloudflare and its AI infrastructure.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, to address security issues, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

7. Data Retention and Security

Product information submitted for label generation is processed to fulfil your request. Whether request data is retained in infrastructure logs or by service providers depends on the applicable configuration and their retention practices.

We aim to use appropriate safeguards to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Avoid entering passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive information into product label fields.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or information about the processing of your personal data. You may also have rights to object to certain processing or withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis.

You can choose what information to submit and can stop using the service at any time. To make a privacy request, contact us using the contact details provided on the Connect Gold website. We may need to verify your request before responding.

9. Children's Privacy

Connectgold Creative is a general-purpose business tool and is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has submitted personal information inappropriately, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. International Processing

Our hosting and AI service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be applied to international data transfers.

11. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their content, security or privacy practices. Review their privacy policies before providing information to them.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the service, its providers or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
