Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Connectgold Creative ("we", "our" or "the service") handles information when you visit our website or use our AI Product Label Studio.

Our website is available at connectgold.sbs. Please read this policy before submitting information to the studio.

1. Information You Submit

Depending on the features you use, you may submit product names, brand names, product categories, descriptions, ingredients or materials, quantities, prices, batch numbers, expiry dates, contact details, label dimensions and design preferences.

Please submit only information necessary to create your label. Do not enter passwords, payment card details, government identity numbers, private customer information or other sensitive information into product description fields.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted to the studio may be used to:

  • Generate suggested product label wording.
  • Display a preview of a label you are creating.
  • Provide label formatting and printing features.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and protect the service.

3. AI Processing and Cloudflare

The studio uses a Cloudflare Worker and may use Cloudflare Workers AI to process information you submit and generate label content. The relevant information is sent to the service endpoint needed to handle your request.

Cloudflare's processing, security systems, operational logs and retention practices are governed by its applicable terms and privacy documentation. Review Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for more information.

We do not promise that submitted information is never logged or retained. Actual handling depends on the service configuration and applicable provider practices.

4. Label Previews and Printing

The studio may display generated text in your browser and use your browser's print function to print a label. Printing may involve your browser, operating system, printer software or connected printing service.

Review the information on a label before printing or distributing it. Do not rely on AI-generated wording as legal, medical, safety or regulatory advice.

5. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

The studio page may not require cookies for its core label generation feature. However, the wider website or third-party services integrated into it may use cookies, local storage, analytics technologies or advertising tools.

The technologies actually used depend on the site's current configuration. If analytics, advertising, consent-management tools or other third-party services are added, this policy should be updated to identify them and explain their relevant purposes.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be processed by service providers involved in hosting, AI generation, website delivery, security or other features you choose to use. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not claim that no third party can process your information. We do not sell personal information as a business practice. This statement does not replace any disclosures required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Security

Information may be handled temporarily to fulfil a request or may appear in provider logs or other operational records, depending on system configuration and provider policies. We cannot promise a specific deletion period unless one has been verified and implemented.

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect systems and information. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can choose not to submit optional information and can stop using the studio at any time. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing.

To make a privacy request, use a contact channel published on the official Connectgold website. We must verify that a suitable contact method and process are available before promising a particular response time or outcome.

9. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Do not submit a child's personal information unless you have the necessary legal authority and a valid reason to do so.

10. International Processing

Hosting and AI providers may process information in countries other than the country where you are located. Where required, applicable legal safeguards should be used for such transfers.

11. External Websites

Our pages may link to external websites. We do not control their privacy practices. Review the privacy policies of those services before submitting information to them.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the service, its providers or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, use the official contact information published on Connectgold. Do not assume that a contact address exists unless it is actually published and monitored.

Important notice: This document is a general privacy-policy template, not legal advice or a certification of compliance. Before publishing it, confirm that every statement matches your actual website, Worker, AI settings, logging, analytics, advertising and data-retention practices.
