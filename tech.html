ConnectGold Mediation SDK - Publisher Manual

Non-Technical Operational & Business Guide to Mobile Monetization, Ad Operations, and Revenue Growth.

Platform Goal: To maximize mobile app eCPM yields and fill rates across 30 ad networks using cloud edge intelligence without degrading application speed or user experience.

1. Executive Summary

Mobile ad mediation allows mobile application publishers to connect their ad inventory to multiple advertising demand networks through a single, central integration. Instead of depending on a single ad network (which may run out of ads or offer low prices in certain geographic regions), ConnectGold acts as a smart marketplace manager. Every time an app user reaches an ad opportunity, ConnectGold contacts multiple ad buyers, evaluates their price offers in real-time, and selects the highest bidder to deliver the ad.

2. Core Concepts & Cloud Architecture

What is an Ad Impression?

An impression occurs each time a user successfully views an advertisement within your mobile app. Monetization performance is measured in eCPM (Effective Cost Per Mille), which represents the revenue your application earns for every 1,000 ad impressions delivered.

How Cloud Edge Mediation Works

  1. Ad Opportunity Fired: A user completes a game level, triggers a video player, or opens a new screen in your application.
  2. Cloud Auction Request: ConnectGold contacts its global Cloud Edge servers (powered by Cloudflare Workers and AI decision routing) instantly.
  3. Real-time Waterfall Evaluation: The system analyzes the user's geographic location, device type, historical performance, and current advertiser demand across 30 ad network partners.
  4. Winning Bid Selection: The highest paying ad network is selected within milliseconds and instructed to render its creative to the user.
  5. Fallback Protection (100% Fill Rate Target): If the highest bidder fails to supply an ad, ConnectGold instantly falls back to the second and third priority bidders in fractions of a second so that revenue opportunities are never lost.

5. Privacy, Consumer Protection & Legal Compliance

To ensure your app remains compliant on Google Play Console, Apple App Store, and global regulatory bodies, ConnectGold supports built-in privacy flags:

  • GDPR (European Economic Area): Ensures targeted ads are only served to European users who explicitly provide consent.
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act): Honors "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" preferences for US citizens.
  • COPPA & Google Families Policy: Provides child-directed safety flags to automatically block adult or age-inappropriate advertising in apps built for children or families.

6. What is app-ads.txt and Why is it Mandatory?

app-ads.txt (Authorized Digital Sellers for Mobile Apps) is an Anti-Fraud initiative created by the Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB). It is a public text file placed on your official developer website domain that tells ad buyers, "These specific ad networks are legally authorized to sell advertising space in my app."

Business Impact: Without a valid app-ads.txt file hosted on your domain, major advertisers (including Google, Meta, and Trade Desk) will automatically block or drastically reduce bids on your app, resulting in up to an 80% loss in potential revenue.

7. Revenue Optimization Best Practices

  1. Combine Rewarded and Interstitial Ads: Rewarded video ads drive the highest user satisfaction and eCPM rates, while Interstitial ads capture revenue from non-paying active users.
  2. Avoid Ad Fatigue: Set frequency caps on full-screen interstitial ads (e.g., maximum 1 ad every 2 to 3 minutes) to maintain app retention and user store ratings.
  3. Keep Your app-ads.txt File Up to Date: Ensure your domain website remains active and accurately mirrors ConnectGold's master seller records so all 30 networks can bid at full capacity.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does using 30 ad networks slow down my mobile application?

No. ConnectGold uses Cloud Edge routing. Rather than loading 30 heavy software libraries into your app, the selection process happens in the cloud in milliseconds, keeping your app fast, lightweight, and responsive.

How does ConnectGold handle users who buy a "Remove Ads" subscription?

The SDK includes an automated user status check. When a user purchases an ad-free tier or premium subscription in your app, ConnectGold immediately stops making cloud ad requests for that user session.

How often are ad network rankings updated?

Rankings are recalculated dynamically in real time for every individual ad request based on live market pricing and network fill capabilities.
